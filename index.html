02 · Behind the playlist

From poster to play.

For most acts, the brief was five songs: roughly three catalog favorites and two recent releases. For artists with small catalogs, the sample stopped at verified releases. For paired DJ billings, each artist has a directory entry.

  1. Match the billingsTranscribe the supplied poster and resolve the recording credits. Collective and side-project credits include NCT’s units, Gabriels for Jacob Lusk, and Beastie Boys alongside Mike D’s solo material.
  2. Build and mergePrepare 32 batches of up to 25 songs, then add them to a single Apple Music playlist, Festival Poster • 01.
  3. Verify the exportApple Music showed a repeated block during the build. After removing it, the final export matched all 791 selections with zero duplicate artist/title pairs.

Track links open the Apple Music catalog. Search Festival Poster • 01 in your Music library for the assembled playlist.
