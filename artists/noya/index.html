NOYA

NOYA is Noya Asaraf (נויה אסרף), a DJ who came up through Tel Aviv’s club scene. She mixes house, acid and electronica and started the Gender Blender party series to bring a wider range of artists to the decks.

Coachella bills NOYA on both Fridays. Her SoundCloud DJ set is linked below. This guide has no verified Apple Music tracks for her.
