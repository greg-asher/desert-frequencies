SZA

SZA’s R&B uses conversational writing and shifting song structures. Ctrl and SOS span the intimate sound of “Love Galore” and the darkly playful hook of “Kill Bill.”

A songwriting link across the poster

“Snooze” won Best R&B Song at the 2024 Grammys, with Friday artist Leon Thomas among the credited writers. SZA’s SOS won Best Progressive R&B Album that year.
