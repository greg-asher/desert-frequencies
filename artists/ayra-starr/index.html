Ayra Starr

Ayra Starr grew up between Cotonou, Benin, where she was born, and Lagos, Nigeria. She signed with Mavin Records. “Bloody Samaritan” and “Rush” show her bringing a relaxed melodic delivery to driving Afrobeats production.
