Dinner Party

Dinner Party connects jazz improvisers and hip-hop production. “Sleepless Nights” and “Freeze Tag,” both with Phoelix, foreground the project’s conversational playing and soft-edged vocal arrangements.

Recording project Terrace Martin, Robert Glasper, Kamasi Washington and 9th Wonder. · Source ↗
