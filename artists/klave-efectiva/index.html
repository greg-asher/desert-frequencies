Klave Efectiva

Klave Efectiva is a norteño group from Coachella, California, billed for both Sundays of the festival. The band announced its eight-track debut, Klave En Vivo, Vol. 1, on June 19, 2026. The live set includes “Paso a Paso” and “Chavo Felix.”

Band members are still unverified. The group’s debut announcement tags In the Cloud Studios, and the streaming credits list the group under its collective name.
