Friday · Math rock

Angine de Poitrine

Angine de Poitrine builds instrumental math rock around microtonal guitar and tightly coordinated drumming. The Quebec duo brings a masked stage show to its off-kilter riffs.

Duo Khn de Poitrine (guitar and bass) and Klek de Poitrine (drums). · Source ↗
