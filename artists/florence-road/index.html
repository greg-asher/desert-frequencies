Florence Road

Irish group Florence Road started playing together in school. “Heavy” and “Miss” put strong vocal melodies over guitar arrangements that grow in intensity.

Members Lily Aron (vocals), Emma Brandon (guitar), Ailbhe Barry (bass) and Hannah Kelly (drums). · Source ↗
