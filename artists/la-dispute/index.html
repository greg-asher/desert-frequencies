La Dispute

Grand Rapids band La Dispute couples post-hardcore dynamics with densely narrated lyrics. “King Park” and “Such Small Hands” show how much of the drama comes from the words.

Members Jordan Dreyer (vocals), Brad Vander Lugt (drums), Chad Sterenberg and Corey Stroffolino (guitars), and Adam Vass (bass). · Source ↗
