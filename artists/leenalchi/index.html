Friday · Pansori fusion

LEENALCHI

LEENALCHI reworks Korean pansori storytelling with bass guitars and drums. “Tiger Is Coming” draws its vocal narrative from Sugungga and resets it over a propulsive bass figure.

Founding members include Jang Young-gyu (bass and musical direction) and Ahn Yi-ho (vocals). · Source ↗

Listening notes

Pansori with two bassists

The Seoul group takes its cue from Korean pansori storytelling. Its lineup includes two bassists and four singers, with no guitar. Start with “Tiger Is Coming” and “Here Comes That Crow.”
