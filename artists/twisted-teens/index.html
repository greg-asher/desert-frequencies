Twisted Teens

Twisted Teens brings the scruff of home-recorded punk to country and rock songwriting. “Twisted Teen” and “100 Bill Is Gone!” capture the project’s rough-edged melodies.

Core members Caspian Hollywell (songwriting and vocals) and Ramon “RJ” Santos (steel guitar). · Source ↗
