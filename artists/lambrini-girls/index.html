Lambrini Girls

Brighton band Lambrini Girls makes punk out of pointed social observation and loud, tightly wound playing. “Lads Lads Lads” and “Company Culture” bring both traits to the front.

Members Phoebe Lunny (vocals and guitar) and Selin Macieira-Boşgelmez (bass). · Source ↗
