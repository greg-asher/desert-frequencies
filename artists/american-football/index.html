American Football

American Football formed in Urbana, Illinois. Its self-titled 1999 debut became an emo touchstone through chiming guitar parts, unusual meters and restrained singing.

2026 album lineup Mike Kinsella, Nate Kinsella, Steve Holmes and Steve Lamos. · Source ↗
