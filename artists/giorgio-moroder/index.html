Giorgio Moroder

Italian producer and composer Giorgio Moroder helped shape electronic disco through his work with Donna Summer. He brought the same synthesizer vocabulary into film scores and records such as From Here to Eternity.

A disco trail in the Rock Hall archive

The Rock Hall library credits Moroder and Pete Bellotte as producers on Donna Summer’s Live and More, whose track list includes “I Feel Love.” The playlist starts Moroder’s sample with “Chase.”
