Måneskin

Måneskin came up in Rome and brought guitar rock to a global pop audience after winning Eurovision. Their cover of “Beggin’” and “I Wanna Be Your Slave” emphasize the quartet’s live-band dynamics.

Band lineup Damiano David (vocals), Victoria De Angelis (bass), Thomas Raggi (guitar) and Ethan Torchio (drums). · Source ↗
