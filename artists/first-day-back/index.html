First Day Back

First Day Back plays guitar-led emo with abrupt dynamic shifts. “Paint” and “Sure, Ok,” from Forward, show the band moving between hushed passages and a full-volume release.

Lineup in KZSC’s interview Maggie (vocals, violin and harmonica), Spencer (drums), Nathan and Zion (guitars), and Luke (bass). · Source ↗

The sample draws from the debut catalog. There is no separate older era in the selection.
